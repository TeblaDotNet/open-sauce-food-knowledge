### chicken-breast

Priority: Frequently used cut; clarify preparation qualifiers. Corpus occurrences: 73.

Meat from the breast of a chicken, sold in forms that can differ in bone, skin and portion size. A boneless breast and a bone-in breast half are not the same prepared piece.

Match the cut to the recipe before applying its method. Size and preparation affect cooking, so appearance or a fixed time alone cannot establish doneness; use appropriate poultry guidance and a food thermometer. The ingredient name does not imply that the meat is already cooked.

Sources: [Chicken from Farm to Table](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/poultry/chicken-farm-table).

Qualification: No universal portion size, cooking time or automatic bone/skin removal. Review: provisional 0.1; no human review.

### paprika

Priority: High-use spice with important variety differences. Corpus occurrences: 61.

A red ground spice made from dried peppers. It contributes colour and pepper flavour, but its heat varies with the peppers used; sweet, hot and smoked versions are not identical.

Read the qualifier when a recipe names a particular paprika. A smoked version adds a smoke character that an unsmoked sweet paprika does not supply. The generic name alone establishes neither a fixed heat level nor a universal substitution for cayenne.

Sources: [About Paprika Uses, Pairings and Recipes](https://www.mccormick.com/blogs/how-to/about-paprika-uses-pairings-and-recipes).

Qualification: No assumption that paprika is always mild or smoked; existing identities retained. Review: provisional 0.1; no human review.

### cumin

Priority: High-use seasoning; distinguish form from flavour. Corpus occurrences: 46.

An aromatic spice used whole or ground; ground cumin is made from the seeds. Its warm, earthy flavour appears in meat rubs, bean dishes, vegetable preparations and numerous spice mixtures.

The form matters to handling: a ground spice disperses differently from intact seeds. Follow the recipe’s whole or ground instruction rather than assuming the same spoonful gives the same result. Cumin is an ingredient in some blends, not another name for the complete blend.

Sources: [Ground Cumin](https://www.mccormick.com/products/mccormick-ground-cumin-1-5-oz).

Qualification: Ground product illustrates use; no fixed whole-to-ground conversion. Review: provisional 0.1; no human review.

### nutmeg

The sweetness describes aroma and flavour rather than added sugar. Follow the recipe quantity and specified form: the same ingredient may be grated or supplied ground. A little seasoning has a different role from a bulk ingredient, and no universal amount suits every dish.
