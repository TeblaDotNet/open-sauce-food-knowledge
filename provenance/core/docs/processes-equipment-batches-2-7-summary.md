## Review backlog and boundaries

The drafts still require human review. Regional cookware and tool names are kept separate where equivalence is unproven; contextual links do not imply substitutions. Safety-sensitive processes do not gain preservation schedules or universal temperature/pressure instructions. Broad terms such as electric beaters, tamis and china cap remain unresolved; cold-smoking and flash-frying need focused scope/safety work. Some earlier deferred concrete tools were subsequently researched and added, as the individual ledgers show.
