## New identities

### bench-scraper (equipment)

A rigid, handled blade used to divide dough and clear a work surface. Its straight edge is suited to working on a bench; it is distinct from a flexible bowl scraper.

Category: preparation-tools. Aliases: bench knife. Subtype of scraper. Sources: [King Arthur Baking: Bench Knife](https://shop.kingarthurbaking.com/items/bench-knife)

Boundary/review: No universal operating limits, substitution equivalence or broader aliases inferred. Generated, unchecked draft.

### bowl-scraper (equipment)

A flexible scraper that follows the inside of a bowl to gather sticky dough or mixture. It need not have the rigid blade or handle of a bench scraper.

Category: preparation-tools. Aliases: none. Subtype of scraper. Sources: [King Arthur Baking: Sourdough tools](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/learn/guides/sourdough/tools)

Boundary/review: No universal operating limits, substitution equivalence or broader aliases inferred. Generated, unchecked draft.

### dough-docker (equipment)

A tool that makes repeated impressions or perforations in dough to limit unwanted puffing. It performs docking; a pastry wheel instead cuts along a line.

Category: bakeware. Aliases: none. Sources: [Ateco: Ateco catalogue: pastry wheels and dough docker, pages 73–76](https://www.atecousa.com/assets/ateco_catalog.pdf)

Boundary/review: No universal operating limits, substitution equivalence or broader aliases inferred. Generated, unchecked draft.

### kitchen-timer (equipment)

A device for tracking a cooking or preparation interval, whether separate or built into an appliance. Elapsed time is a cue to check the food, not a measurement of its temperature.

Category: measuring. Aliases: none. Sources: [Land O’Lakes: Kitchen Equipment and Their Uses](https://www.landolakes.com/kitchen-reference/equipment-guide/)

Boundary/review: No universal operating limits, substitution equivalence or broader aliases inferred. Generated, unchecked draft.

### pizza-peel (equipment)

A broad, handled tool for supporting and moving pizza into or out of an oven. It transfers the pizza rather than providing the stationary baking surface.

Category: bakeware. Aliases: none. Sources: [General Mills Foodservice: General Mills Pizza Guide: pizza tools](https://www.generalmillsfoodservice.com/-/media/files/industry-resources/pizzeria/crust-tips-and-resources/pizza-interactive-product-guide.ashx)

### baking-steel (equipment)

A steel baking surface that stores heat and transfers it to dough in contact with it. Its heat transfer differs from a baking stone, so the two are not simply interchangeable materials.

Category: bakeware. Aliases: pizza steel. Sources: [King Arthur Baking: Pizza steel vs. stone](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2026/07/17/pizza-steel-vs-stone)

### pastry-wheel (equipment)

A handled cutting wheel for dough or pastry. Plain and fluted edges produce different cut edges; the tool is distinct from a pastry blender that cuts fat into flour.

Category: bakeware. Aliases: none. Sources: [Ateco: Ateco catalogue: pastry wheels and dough docker, pages 73–76](https://www.atecousa.com/assets/ateco_catalog.pdf)

### piping-bag (equipment)

A tapered bag for dispensing soft mixtures such as icing, cream or mashed potato. A fitted tip can shape the output; piping is not limited to sweet decorations.

Category: bakeware. Aliases: pastry bag. Sources: [Land O’Lakes: Kitchen Equipment and Their Uses](https://www.landolakes.com/kitchen-reference/equipment-guide/)

### offset-spatula (equipment)

A spreading spatula whose blade is offset from the handle. The clearance helps spread icing or batter without the handle being in the same plane as the food.

Category: bakeware. Aliases: none. Subtype of spatula. Sources: [King Arthur Baking: Why an offset spatula is an essential baking tool](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2022/12/09/baking-with-offset-spatula)

## Existing drafts improved

- **equipment/scraper:** A bench knife can portion a dough into pieces before shaping. The scraper name alone does not specify a cutting edge suitable for that job; choose the rigid bench form when portioning is the task. Source: [King Arthur Baking: Bench Knife](https://shop.kingarthurbaking.com/items/bench-knife). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/grater:** A rasp form gives access to a small curved surface such as citrus peel. That access is a separate consideration from the size of its teeth; grater shape and the fineness of the result are not the same property. Source: [Microplane: Premium Classic Zester Grater](https://www.microplaneintl.com/en/our-products/premium-classic-series/zester-grater). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/zester:** A rasp-style zester can also grate hard cheese. This overlap explains why zesting tools appear among graters without making every grater a tool for removing only the outer citrus peel. Source: [Microplane: Premium Classic Zester Grater](https://www.microplaneintl.com/en/our-products/premium-classic-series/zester-grater). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/pasta-machine:** The roller description above concerns the sheet-making form. A separate cutter determines noodle width; changing roller thickness does not change the width of a cutter. Extrusion machines are another form of pasta machine, not another setting on a sheet roller. Source: [KitchenAid: Pasta Roller KSMPSA](https://www.kitchenaid.com/countertop-appliances/stand-mixers/attachments/p.pasta-roller.KSMPSA.html). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/rolling-pin:** End guides can limit the thickness of a dough sheet, but they do not guarantee a chosen diameter. If a dough portion reaches the guide thickness first, its final outline may remain smaller than the recipe requires. Source: [King Arthur Baking: The best rolling pin, based on what you like to bake](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2024/10/30/best-rolling-pin). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/kitchen-shears:** Snipping herbs, cutting kitchen twine and shaping parchment are different uses of the same shears. A pull-apart joint aids access for cleaning, but it is a design feature to check rather than a property of every pair. Source: [Williams Sonoma: WÜSTHOF Pull-Apart Shears](https://www.williams-sonoma.com/products/wusthof-pull-apart-kitchen-shears/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/chopping-board:** Deep grooves and excessive wear can make a board difficult to clean effectively. Cleaning routines do not remove that physical damage; USDA advises replacing worn boards with hard-to-clean grooves. Source: [USDA FSIS: Cutting Boards](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/cutting-boards). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/knife:** Separate usable vegetable trim from waste as you cut. Squaring food for regular cuts creates offcuts that may suit stock or sauce; a precise presentation need not mean discarding all the remaining edible material. Source: [Culinary Institute of America / David Kamen: Five Knife Skills Every Chef Must Have!](https://www.ciachef.edu/blog/5-knife-skills-every-chef-must/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/paring-knife:** Peeling and trimming should remove the unwanted layer without taking an unnecessary amount of flesh. A short blade can work around small irregular areas; a large, tough rind calls for a different tool choice. Source: [Culinary Institute of America: Home School: Prepping Vegetables](https://www.ciafoodies.com/home-school-prepping-vegetables/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/sieve:** A support loop lets the illustrated strainer rest across a bowl while liquid drains. This support arrangement is separate from mesh fineness: both the opening size and how the sieve sits on the receiving vessel matter. Source: [OXO: 8-inch Double Rod Strainer](https://www.oxo.com/8-strainer.html). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/chop:** Rough chopping still benefits from reasonably similar piece sizes when the pieces will cook together. Irregular outlines and wildly different sizes are separate issues; a rustic cut need not mean uneven doneness. Source: [Culinary Institute of America: Home School: Prepping Vegetables](https://www.ciafoodies.com/home-school-prepping-vegetables/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/dice:** Brunoise is a particular small, regular dice. The general instruction to dice does not automatically request brunoise, so preserve any size cue in the recipe rather than inferring the finest cut. Source: [MICHELIN Guide / Rachel Tan: Kitchen Language: Know Your Knife Cuts](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/dining-in/knife-cut-types). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/mince:** For herbs, an initial coarse cut can be followed by repeated cuts to reach the intended fineness. Mincing does not require preserving the regular faces of a dice; its endpoint is the small-piece texture required by the dish. Source: [Culinary Institute of America: Home School: Prepping Vegetables](https://www.ciafoodies.com/home-school-prepping-vegetables/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/julienne:** Cross-cutting julienned strips produces brunoise. Batonnet stops at a thicker stick; these names distinguish the resulting shapes and sizes, not different knives that must be purchased. Source: [MICHELIN Guide / Rachel Tan: Kitchen Language: Know Your Knife Cuts](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/dining-in/knife-cut-types). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/slice:** Slicing can be an intermediate stage before sticks or dice. The first slices set one dimension of the later pieces, so inconsistent thickness at that stage carries through to the finished cut. Source: [Culinary Institute of America: Home School: Prepping Vegetables](https://www.ciafoodies.com/home-school-prepping-vegetables/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/grate:** Fine grating can distribute hard cheese or citrus peel as small particles rather than visible strips. A tool capable of both tasks does not make their ingredients interchangeable; the recipe still determines what is being grated. Source: [Microplane: Premium Classic Zester Grater](https://www.microplaneintl.com/en/our-products/premium-classic-series/zester-grater). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/zest:** Zesting keeps the coloured peel, whereas supreming citrus keeps the flesh and removes peel and segment membranes. These preparations use different parts of the fruit and should not be treated as alternate names for peeling. Source: [Noble Citrus: How to Cut Citrus for Salads with Chef Justin](https://noblecitrus.com/recipes/how-to-cut-citrus-for-salads-with-chef-justin/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/fold:** Folding pastry into layers during lamination is a different context from gently folding a foam mixture. A recipe must supply that context; repeated pastry folds are not an instruction to beat or stir a foam. Source: [King Arthur Baking: How we make puff pastry at the King Arthur bakery](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2013/08/02/puff-pastry-layer-it-like-the-pros). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/dock:** A dough docker repeats the puncturing action across a sheet. Its purpose differs from the continuous cut made by a pastry wheel: cutting the outline of a pastry piece does not dock its surface. Source: [Ateco: Ateco catalogue: pastry wheels and dough docker, pages 73–76](https://www.atecousa.com/assets/ateco_catalog.pdf). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/laminate:** Rolling sets the dimensions of the sheet between folds. A pin with thickness guides controls one dimension, not the full rectangle, so check the required shape as well as thickness when building layers. Source: [King Arthur Baking: The best rolling pin, based on what you like to bake](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2024/10/30/best-rolling-pin). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
