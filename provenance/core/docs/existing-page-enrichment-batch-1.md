### equipment: oven

Priority: Most-used unenriched equipment; mode distinctions matter. Corpus occurrences: 286.

An enclosed heated space used for baking and roasting. A convection setting adds circulating air; it is a mode of heat delivery, not a different recipe method.

Moving air can change browning and the rate of baking. Fan strength and oven design vary, so a convection setting is not a universal improvement or a fixed temperature conversion for every bake.

Sources: [Convection oven baking: Why this baker is a fan](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2025/03/19/convection-oven-baking).

### equipment: pan

Priority: Very frequent broad vessel term needing qualification. Corpus occurrences: 256.

A broad cookware term whose practical meaning depends on the vessel. A shallow skillet offers access for turning and searing; a deeper saucepan holds liquids more readily.

Choose by the operation, usable surface area and sides rather than the word pan alone. A wide braising vessel and a small saucepan are not interchangeable merely because both can be covered and heated.

### equipment: lid

### equipment: strainer

### equipment: spatula

Spatula covers several working shapes. A flexible silicone head can scrape a bowl or mix a batter; an offset metal blade is especially useful for spreading icing.

### process: sieve

Pass material through a mesh. For dry baking ingredients such as flour, cocoa or powdered sugar, sifting can break up lumps and loosen the powder.

### process: pan-fry

Priority: Distinguish partial immersion from deep frying. Corpus occurrences: 1.

Fry food in a pan with fat reaching only partway up the food, turning as needed to cook the other side.

Unlike deep-frying, the food is not surrounded by fat throughout. Batch size matters because adding too much food can cool the fat; follow the recipe's method rather than treating pan-frying and deep-frying as interchangeable.

Sources: [Tips for Frying on the Stove](https://www.ciafoodies.com/tips-for-frying-on-the-stove/).

### process: scramble

Priority: Distinctive egg-curd process. Corpus occurrences: 2.

Cook beaten eggs while moving the setting mixture with a utensil, drawing and folding it into curds.

Beating combines the raw egg; scrambling also involves heat and the movement of the setting egg. The size and texture of the curds depend on that handling, so an instruction to beat eggs alone does not describe scrambling.

Sources: [Basic Scrambled Eggs](https://www.incredibleegg.org/recipe/basic-scrambled-eggs/).
