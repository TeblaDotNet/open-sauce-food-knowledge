### process: slash

Priority: Useful bread-surface distinction; paired with existing scoring knowledge. Corpus occurrences: 1.

Make an incision in the surface of food. For bread, slashing the dough before baking creates an opening that helps direct expansion as the loaf heats.

A sharp blade limits dragging through the dough. Placement, angle and depth depend on the loaf and intended result, so a decorative mark and a functional expansion cut need not be identical. This is different from piercing many small holes with a fork.

Sources: [Baking trials: Just how much does scoring impact bread?](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2024/05/30/scoring-bread-dough).

Qualification: Bread-focused example, not a universal score/slash identity merge. Review: provisional 0.1; no human review.
