## New identities

### rice-cooker (equipment)

An appliance designed to cook rice with controlled heating. Basic models and programmed models manage different parts of soaking, cooking and steaming; a rice-cooker label does not imply pressure cooking or every grain setting.

Category: appliances. Aliases: none. Sources: [Zojirushi: Rice cooker FAQ](https://www.zojirushi.com/app/faq/rice-cookers)

Boundary/review: No universal operating limits, substitution guarantees or broader aliases inferred. Generated, unchecked draft.

### tandoor (equipment)

A deep clay-lined oven used for cooking foods on skewers and breads against the heated inner wall. Commercial examples use different fuels and housings; this identity does not establish installation requirements or equivalence with other regional ovens.

Category: cookware. Aliases: tandoor oven. Subtype of oven. Sources: [GULATI: Tandoori clay ovens](https://www.tandoors.com/)

Boundary/review: No universal operating limits, substitution guarantees or broader aliases inferred. Generated, unchecked draft.

### tortilla-press (equipment)

A hinged pair of plates with a lever for flattening portions of dough. Pressing shapes the tortilla before cooking; the hand press is not itself a heated griddle.

Category: preparation-tools. Aliases: none. Sources: [Victoria Cookware: Tortilla press instructions](https://victoriacookware.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/instructions-for-the-victoria-cast-iron-tortilla-press.pdf)

### bamboo-steamer (equipment)

A bamboo steaming basket with a slatted base, commonly used with a cover and sometimes in tiers. It supports food in steam rather than being the vessel that boils the water.

Category: cookware. Aliases: none. Subtype of steaming-basket. Sources: [Town Food Service: 3 Piece Bamboo Steamer Set](https://townfood.com/product/bamboo-steamer-set-3-piece/)

### spider-strainer (equipment)

A long-handled open wire basket for lifting food from cooking liquid while allowing liquid to drain. Its coarse, open structure serves a different task from fine filtration.

Category: preparation-tools. Aliases: spider skimmer. Subtype of strainer. Sources: [OXO: Scoop And Strain Skimmer](https://www.oxo.com/oxo-good-grips-scoop-and-strain-skimmer.html)

Category: bakeware. Aliases: none. Subtype of cutter. Sources: [King Arthur Baking: Decorating Cookies](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/learn/guides/cookie-decorating)

### lard (process)

Insert strips of fat into a piece of meat, often with a larding needle. This puts fat within the meat rather than covering its outside with a sheet of fat.

Category: preparation-and-cutting. Aliases: none. Sources: [Chef Simon: Larder](https://chefsimon.com/articles/lexique-larder)

Boundary/review: No universal operating limits, substitution guarantees or broader aliases inferred. Generated, unchecked draft.

### bard (process)

Cover a piece of meat or other food with thin sheets of fat before cooking. The fat is outside the food; larding instead inserts strips into it.

Category: preparation-and-cutting. Aliases: none. Sources: [Chef Simon: Barder](https://chefsimon.com/articles/lexique-barder)

Boundary/review: No universal operating limits, substitution guarantees or broader aliases inferred. Generated, unchecked draft.

### cook-en-papillote (process)

Category: moist-heat-cooking. Aliases: none. Sources: [Chef Simon: Pliage d’une papillote](https://chefsimon.com/gourmets/chef-simon/recettes/pliage-d-une-papillote)

## Existing drafts improved

- **equipment/colander:** A colander receives food and liquid poured into it. A spider strainer instead lifts portions out of the cooking vessel, leaving most liquid in place. These are different ways to drain food, even if either can handle a large pasta shape. Source: [OXO: Scoop And Strain Skimmer](https://www.oxo.com/oxo-good-grips-scoop-and-strain-skimmer.html). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/steaming-basket:** Bamboo dim sum sets may combine several baskets and a separate cover. The basket, lid and water-heating vessel are distinct parts of the arrangement; selecting a basket alone does not establish the fit of the complete stack. Source: [Town Food Service: 3 Piece Bamboo Steamer Set](https://townfood.com/product/bamboo-steamer-set-3-piece/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/baking-paper:** Baking parchment can form a folded cooking parcel as well as line a tray. In a papillote, the joined edges help retain moisture; laying a loose sheet over food does not reproduce the same enclosure. Source: [Chef Simon: Pliage d’une papillote](https://chefsimon.com/gourmets/chef-simon/recettes/pliage-d-une-papillote). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/butchers-twine:** Twine can hold an external layer of fat against a roast during barding. Its role there is fastening the covering, whereas larding introduces fat into the meat itself. The same string can support different preparations without making them equivalent. Source: [Chef Simon: Barder](https://chefsimon.com/articles/lexique-barder). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/pie-dish:** The dish supports the pastry through either a partial or a full preliminary bake. Which endpoint is needed depends on whether the filling will be baked later; the vessel alone does not determine that sequence. Source: [King Arthur Baking School: Holiday Pie Workbook: partial and full baking](https://shop.kingarthurbaking.com/content/class-pdfs/Holiday-Pie-Workbook-King-Arthur-Baking.pdf). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/pastry-brush:** Applying egg wash changes the finish of the dough surface. The brush distributes the chosen wash, but it does not determine its ingredients: retain the recipe’s wash composition rather than treating every brushing liquid as egg wash. Source: [King Arthur Baking School: Fruit Pies Workbook: egg wash](https://shop.kingarthurbaking.com/content/class-pdfs/Fruit-Pies-Workbook-King-Arthur-Baking.pdf). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/electric-kettle:** A stovetop kettle requires an external burner or hob; an electric kettle contains its own heating system. A whistling lid describes a signal mechanism, not a shared heating method or proof that the vessels can be exchanged between heat sources. Source: [Le Creuset: Classic Whistling Kettle](https://www.lecreuset.com/classic-whistling-kettle-matte-white/40114260431241.html). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/thermometer:** A stem thermometer may sense along a length of probe rather than only at its tip. The displayed number can therefore represent a region of food; follow the instrument’s sensing-area guidance instead of assuming that touching a point measures only that point. Source: [USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service: Food Thermometers](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/food-thermometers). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/skewer:** In a tandoor, skewers support food within a deep heated chamber. That use needs a length and construction suited to the oven; a short serving pick and a cooking skewer do not become interchangeable because both are pointed rods. Source: [GULATI: Tandoori clay ovens](https://www.tandoors.com/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **equipment/food-mill:** Changing the perforated disc changes the texture of what passes through: the documented mill uses different discs for smooth sauces and coarser potato preparations. This is mechanical selection, not merely running the same blade for longer. Source: [OXO: Food Mill Use and Care](https://www.oxo.com/media/wysiwyg/PDF_Files/1071478_foodmill.pdf). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/blind-bake:** Par-baking also applies to foods such as bread rolls. Blind-baking describes the absence of the eventual filling, while par-baking describes an incomplete bake; an empty crust may be either partly or fully baked. Source: [King Arthur Baking: Homemade Brown and Serve Rolls](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/homemade-brown-and-serve-rolls-recipe). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/bake:** A cake’s surface colour, spring and interior crumb provide different cues. Inserting a tester inspects a small part of the crumb; read that result with the recipe’s other cues rather than treating one clean probe as a universal endpoint for every baked food. Source: [King Arthur Baking: How to tell when cake is done](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2019/07/01/how-to-tell-when-cake-is-done). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/fillet:** Pin-boning can follow the removal of a fillet. It removes small bones retained within the flesh rather than separating the main slab from the skeleton; the two operations belong to different stages of fish preparation. Source: [Taylor Foods / Direct Seafoods: Fish preparation and filleting guide](https://www.taylorfoods.co.uk/our-seafood/preparation/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/debone:** Fish pin-boning is one narrower deboning task. It can involve extracting individual small bones from an already cut fillet, unlike cutting an intact piece of meat away from a large central bone. Source: [Taylor Foods / Direct Seafoods: Fish preparation and filleting guide](https://www.taylorfoods.co.uk/our-seafood/preparation/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/steam:** A covered bamboo basket is one equipment arrangement for steaming. Its slatted base admits steam while holding the food; the basket is not immersed like the food in a pot of boiling water. Source: [Town Food Service: 3 Piece Bamboo Steamer Set](https://townfood.com/product/bamboo-steamer-set-3-piece/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/blanch:** A lifting strainer can remove a batch of food while retaining the blanching water in the pot. This differs from draining the whole pot through a colander and can be useful when the recipe calls for successive batches. Source: [OXO: Scoop And Strain Skimmer](https://www.oxo.com/oxo-good-grips-scoop-and-strain-skimmer.html). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/stir-fry:** Velveting can prepare a protein before the final stir-fry. The initial treatment and the later mixing with vegetables or sauce are separate stages; a finished stir-fry need not obtain all of its texture from the final toss in the wok. Source: [The Woks of Life: How to Velvet Chicken for Stir-fry](https://thewoksoflife.com/how-to-velvet-chicken-stir-fry/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/marinate:** A velveting marinade may include starch as part of a coating before cooking. This is a particular texture-oriented use of marinating, not a reason to add starch or baking soda to every marinade. Source: [The Woks of Life: How to Velvet Chicken for Stir-fry](https://thewoksoflife.com/how-to-velvet-chicken-stir-fry/). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/score:** Bread scoring creates planned places for a loaf’s surface to open during oven expansion. A bread lame provides a controlled blade for that purpose; scoring the surface does not divide the dough into separate portions. Source: [King Arthur Baking: Bread Slashing Lame](https://shop.kingarthurbaking.com/items/bread-slashing-lame). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.
- **process/truss:** Trussing holds the shape of a bird, whereas tying on a bard holds an external covering of fat. Both can use kitchen twine, but the shared fastening tool does not make barding and trussing the same preparation. Source: [Chef Simon: Barder](https://chefsimon.com/articles/lexique-barder). Existing draft improved; not newly human reviewed.

## Deferred and bounded decisions

- **tamis:** French usage is broader than English drum-sieve usage. Defer a scoped drum-sieve identity pending focused evidence; do not alias every sieve.
- **china cap / chinois:** Names overlap across markets and mesh/perforation forms. No blanket china-cap alias or fixed aperture asserted.
- **probe thermometer:** Probes can measure food or ambient conditions. Use food probe thermometer; do not claim the unqualified term as its alias.
- **tandir / tonir / tannour:** Manufacturer marketing groups related regional ovens. Regional identity equivalence is not established here.
- **flash-fry / cold-smoke:** Method boundaries and safety context need further evidence; no timings or preservation claims added.
- **larding needle / fish-bone tweezers:** Potential next equipment identities; no ad hoc generic needle or tweezers alias added.
