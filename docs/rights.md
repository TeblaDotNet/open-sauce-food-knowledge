## Publication gate and practical review

The unresolved scope is article prose across the 911-record collection: existing citations and review version 0.1 do not supply a complete block-level author/rightsholder chain. This finding does not assert that every article infringes, nor that every article is cleared. No per-article clearance list can be justified from the current records alone.

For an explicit reuse grant or a flagged publication-risk case, record the relevant article/block author or contributor authority, independent authorship versus quotation/adaptation, source terms, permission or applicable exception, and reviewer/date/evidence. Seek the actual rightsholder decision where needed. If clearance cannot be established, separately approve a replacement written independently from facts or exclude the disputed expression from publication. Do not silently relabel or rewrite it as part of migration.

A citation identifies evidence; it does not grant permission to reproduce expressive wording. Individual photograph metadata remains an evidence record to verify, not a new licence grant or fresh external rights audit. No external source verification was performed in this documentation pass. Preserved source-specific terms and historical notices continue to apply.
