For CC BY material, credit **Tebla and Open Sauce Food contributors**, identify the
project and source revision, link CC BY 4.0 and identify changes. Preserve supplied
source-author and photograph credits separately. CC0 does not waive rights in
third-party expression merely because it appears in a structured field.

## Article provenance limitation

The existing records supply citations, draft review status and historical project
context, but do not provide individual contributor/rightsholder attestations for
all 911 articles. This migration cannot confidently assert a complete rights chain
for every block. The original declarations and citations are retained, and that
question is recorded in [rights findings](docs/rights.md). No imported or uncertain
text is newly declared CC BY merely by copying it into this repository.

Every article remains draft/generated review version 0.1. Licence metadata and
review status are different questions; neither constitutes independent human
review of culinary advice or third-party rights.

## Images and archived material

All 349 images retain exact bytes, creators, source URLs, licence links, changes
and article associations. Their individual public-domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA
claims are preserved, not replaced with the repository's prose/data terms.
Selection records include rejected candidates and remain intact.

The `provenance/core/` tree is an immutable source archive. Its documents and code
retain original scopes, context and relative references, which may refer to paths
in the original Core repository. They are not fresh claims about this new layout.
Additional archived licence texts preserve historical notices; their presence does
not impose a blanket licence on unrelated new tooling.
